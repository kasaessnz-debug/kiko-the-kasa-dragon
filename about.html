About Kiko

A kinder, brighter Australia — one smile at a time.

Read Kiko’s storyBook Kiko

Hello from Kiko

A friendly, playful and caring character.

Kiko — The KASA Dragon was created to bring smiles, spark imagination and encourage kindness.

Whether Kiko is meeting children, appearing at a community event, cheering at a sporting occasion or exploring somewhere new, every Kiko appearance is about creating positive memories.

“Kind little moments can make a big difference.”— Kiko

Kiko’s story

Kiko believes even the smallest act of kindness can make someone’s day a little brighter. That simple idea sits at the heart of Kiko’s world.

Kiko was created as more than a mascot. The long-term vision is to build a recognisable Australian character connected with children, families, community, adventure and giving back.

As Kiko explores Australia, the story will continue to grow through events, adventures, community partnerships, storytelling and new experiences.

What Kiko stands for

Kindness

Small acts can create meaningful change.

Community

Bringing people together and celebrating the communities around us.

Fun

Creating happy, colourful and memorable experiences.

Brighter Futures

Encouraging children to dream big, be confident and believe in what is possible.

Kiko & KASA

Kiko — The KASA Dragon was created by KASA Essentials as part of a long-term vision to create something that goes beyond products.

Kiko represents the community, family and imagination side of KASA. While KASA Essentials and Kiko remain connected, Kiko is growing into his own recognisable character and brand.

Same little dragon. Bigger possibilities.

Follow Kiko as he explores schools, festivals, sport and special places across Australia.

Booking questions

How much does it cost to book Kiko?

Every event is different, so pricing depends on your location, date, how long you would like Kiko to stay and what you would like Kiko to do. Send an enquiry and we’ll come back to you with availability and a quote.

Where can Kiko appear?

Kiko appears at events across Australia. Let us know your venue and suburb in the enquiry form and we’ll confirm what’s possible for your location.

What kinds of events can Kiko attend?

Schools, community festivals, sporting events, shopping centres, council events, family days, corporate family events, charity events, fundraisers, cultural celebrations, promotional appearances and special occasions.

What can Kiko do at my event?

Depending on the event and agreed package, a Kiko appearance can include meet-and-greets, photo opportunities, audience interaction, event walk-arounds, stage appearances, community engagement, brand or event photography, social-media content and special themed appearances.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. Submitting an enquiry does not confirm a booking. We’ll reply with availability and a quote, and your date is secured once the booking is confirmed — usually with a deposit.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends, school holidays and the festive season fill up first, so get your enquiry in once you have a date.

Can Kiko support a charity or school initiative?

Yes — giving back is at the heart of Kiko. Tell us about your initiative through the contact page and choose “Charity / Kiko Gives Back” or “School Enquiry”.

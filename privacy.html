Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy should be reviewed by KASA Essentials before launch.

Kiko — The KASA Dragon is created and owned by KASA Essentials Pty Ltd (“we”, “us”). We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you send a booking enquiry, contact message or sign up for updates, we collect the details you give us — such as your name, organisation, email, phone number and event details. We also record basic information about how you found the site (for example the referring website or campaign link). Deposit payments are processed by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card details.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes, manage bookings and deposits, send updates you asked for, and improve how we reach families and organisations. We don’t sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the site and bookings — such as our website host (Netlify) and payment processor (Stripe) — and only as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

You can ask to access or correct your information, or unsubscribe from updates at any time, by contacting us through the contact page.

Children

Our forms are intended for parents, guardians, schools and organisations. Please don’t submit children’s personal information through the site.

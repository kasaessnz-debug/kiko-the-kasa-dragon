Kiko Gives Back

Stronger communities. Brighter futures.

Get involvedSee our initiatives

Because a kinder tomorrow matters

Kindness has the power to make a real difference.

Through Kiko Gives Back, we aim to support children, families, charities, community organisations and initiatives creating positive outcomes across Australia.

Kiko Gives Back is the community side of Kiko’s journey — using Kiko’s growing visibility to create smiles, support worthwhile causes, bring people together and help organisations doing meaningful work.

Where Kiko can help

Supporting children & families

Where appropriate, Kiko can support organisations and initiatives helping children and families through difficult circumstances — including hospital-related activities, family programs, special events and community initiatives.

Schools & education

Kiko believes children should be encouraged to dream, learn, explore and treat others with kindness — through positive appearances, community programs, reading activities, kindness initiatives and educational experiences.

Make a school enquiry

Community together

Kiko is open to partnerships with local community groups, charities, councils, schools and organisations creating stronger and more connected communities.

Community events

Local festivals, council events, cultural celebrations, community days, family events, charity events, fundraising activities and sporting community events.

Invite Kiko to your event

Giving with purpose

As Kiko grows, giving back grows with him. The long-term aim is for community involvement to be a permanent part of the Kiko story — not an occasional activity.

How you can help

There are many ways organisations, businesses and communities can support Kiko Gives Back.

  • Partnering with Kiko
  • Supporting a community initiative
  • Sponsoring an appearance
  • Inviting Kiko to a community event
  • Collaborating on a charity activity
  • Supporting a school initiative
  • Providing products or services
  • Introducing Kiko to organisations doing meaningful work

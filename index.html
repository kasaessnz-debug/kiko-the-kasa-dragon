Good questions.

How much does Kiko cost?

Quotes start from $199. The price depends on how long Kiko stays, travel to your suburb and what you’d like him to do. You get it in writing before you book.

Where does Kiko go?

Kiko is based in Melbourne and visits events across Australia. Tell us your suburb and we’ll work travel into your quote.

Who looks after the kids?

Children stay with a parent, teacher or event staff the whole time, and Kiko’s performer is never left alone with children.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends, school holidays and fête season fill first. Check your date as soon as you have one.

What if plans change?

More than 14 days out, you can move or cancel. The details are in our booking terms.

Got a date?

Tell us when and where. We’ll reply with availability and a quote.
